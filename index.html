Who we are

Built to be tested.
Not just advertised.

Black Steel Ballistics is an independent media project covering firearms-related equipment, accessories and the people who make them. Our aim is to help viewers make better-informed decisions through balanced reviews, clear explanations and honest assessments.

We are not affiliated with a manufacturer unless explicitly disclosed. Product loans, gifts, sponsorships or other material connections will be identified in relevant coverage.
